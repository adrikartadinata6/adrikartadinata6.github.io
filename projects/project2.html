Furnace Burner Control System

Industrial Automation Dec 2025 s.d. Present Furnace Operation & Burner Control Engineer

Engineered and commissioned Omron PLC-based furnace control programs for LPG burner systems, improving temperature regulation accuracy across heat-soaking zones. Designed electrical wiring layouts to PUIL/IEC 60364 standards and executed castable refractory curing cycles.

Overview

Project Overview

Background / Problem Statement

Furnace panels running on manual burner control needed tighter, more consistent temperature regulation during heat-soaking, and castable refractory curing was exposed to thermal-shock cracking without controlled temperature staging.

Project Objectives

Improve temperature regulation accuracy across heat-soaking zones, reduce the need for manual operator intervention during hold periods, and prevent thermal-shock-induced cracking in completed refractory components.

Main Idea / System Concept

An Omron PLC-based control program drives the LPG burner system through staged temperature profiles, paired with wiring layouts designed to PUIL/IEC 60364 standards and continuous LPG supply pressure monitoring across every burner line.

Application / Use Case

LPG-fired industrial furnace operation and castable refractory curing at PT Aldzama's furnace automation and refractory production lines.

Technical Details

Technical Details

The PLC program sequences burner ignition and temperature staging across heat-soaking zones, while wiring and pressure monitoring keep the panel safe and compliant during every production shift.
