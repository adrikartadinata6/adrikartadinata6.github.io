Kiln Demolition, PT Hensheng New Energy

Site Supervision May 2026 Project Supervisor

Spearheaded on-site supervision of kiln demolition workflows, directing field technicians and subcontractor teams to deliver full scope on schedule with a verified zero-LTI safety record, in full conformance with Kemenaker RI K3/HSE regulations.

Overview

Project Overview

Background / Problem Statement

PT Hensheng New Energy Material Indonesia required a kiln demolished safely and on schedule, coordinating in-house field technicians alongside subcontractor teams under strict K3/HSE oversight.

Project Objectives

Deliver the full demolition scope on schedule, maintain a verified zero-LTI safety record, and keep management informed with real-time progress and safety visibility.

Main Idea / System Concept

Direct on-site supervision of field technicians and subcontractor teams, backed by a structured daily reporting framework covering work progress, material consumption, and safety observations.

Application / Use Case

Full-scope kiln demolition works at a PT Hensheng New Energy Material Indonesia industrial site.

Technical Details

Technical Details

Delivery was managed through direct field supervision paired with a structured reporting system, so schedule, material use and safety status stayed visible to management every day.

Workflow Explanation

1

Mobilization

Directed field technicians and subcontractor teams at mobilization, aligning scope, schedule and K3/HSE requirements.

2

Daily supervision & reporting

Engineered a structured daily reporting framework integrating work-progress metrics, material consumption data, and safety observations for real-time management visibility.

3

K3/HSE enforcement

Enforced K3/HSE compliance protocols at every phase in full conformance with Kemenaker RI regulations.

4

Demobilization

Closed out the full scope on schedule, maintaining zero reportable incidents from mobilization through demobilization.

Daily reporting records and K3/HSE documentation for this project are internal to PT Aldzama and PT Hensheng New Energy. Available on request.
